# Schema Registry Specification Decisions

This register records the observable Avro, JSON Schema, Protobuf, and portable
identity choices implemented by the root module. Source and update pins are in
[`spec/sources.lock.json`](../spec/sources.lock.json); executable mappings are
in the [conformance matrix](../specification/README.md). A changed decision
requires compatibility, changelog, conformance, and provider-boundary review.
Superseded entries remain here and link to their replacements.

## SCHEMA-REG-DEC-004: Portable fingerprints remain distinct from provider identities
